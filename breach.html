the third player

The network fights back.

This isn't a static map with a scoreboard. Security systems are emergent — they watch what the red team does, adapt their rules, and start setting traps of their own. A door you breached last match may be sealed this one. A patch you thought held may be the one that's watching you.

So the question isn't just "can you break in?" It's "what counts as trespassing when the doors think for themselves?"
