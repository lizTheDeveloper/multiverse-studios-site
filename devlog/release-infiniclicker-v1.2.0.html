Infiniclicker v1.2.0 — The Checkout That Actually Works

June 8, 2026 INFINICLICKER RELEASE

This is the one where we stop losing every single purchase attempt. Infiniclicker v1.2.0 replaces the browser prompt() dialog — the thing that made every PWYC checkout look like a phishing popup — with a proper styled price selector that matches the Bloomberg Terminal aesthetic.

We also added five new Belt-lore cosmetic skins at $2–$10 price points, a leaderboard system, daily challenges, and a supporter recognition badge. But the price selector is the headline. Sixteen checkout sessions, zero conversions. That era is over.

What's New

  • Styled PWYC price selector — preset buttons ($2, $5, $8, custom) replace the browser prompt. Looks like it belongs in the game.
  • 5 Belt-lore cosmetic skins — faction-themed skins expanding the cosmetic catalog.
  • Belt Exchange Rankings — anonymous leaderboard with share-your-rank.
  • Daily challenges — rotating objectives with bonus rewards.
  • Supporter recognition — badge, social proof counter, and thank-you modal for supporters.

Fixes

  • Checkout now redirects back to the game after Stripe payment.
  • All available payment methods enabled in Checkout Sessions.

Play Infiniclicker — it's free, and if you like it, the price selector actually works now.
