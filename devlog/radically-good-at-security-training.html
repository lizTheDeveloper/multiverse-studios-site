Radically Good at Security Training

September 24, 2026 Multiverse Studios Reclaim City By the Numbers

Security training usually means a video you half-watch and a quiz you click through. Reclaim City does it differently. Every mission is a real thing you do to a real account — check it for breaches, change the password, turn on two-factor, pull your data back from a broker — and each one you finish takes back a building in a city the data brokers own.

We launched it on September 7th. Eighteen days later, here’s what players did.

Why this matters

Most people know they should turn on two-factor and freeze their credit. Knowing isn’t the problem. Getting around to it is. Reclaim City turns each of those chores into a building you take back, and it’s honest about why it matters: the accounts are yours, and the breaches are real.

Eighteen thousand actions is eighteen thousand fewer open doors. Every password changed after a breach is one a scammer can’t reuse. Every credit freeze is a loan that can’t be opened in someone’s name. None of it is flashy. All of it is real, and nobody had to sit through a slideshow to do it.

We think that’s radical. We also think it’s just good security training.

— Multiverse Studios
