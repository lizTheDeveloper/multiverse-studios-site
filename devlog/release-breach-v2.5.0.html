BREACH.MMO v2.5 — 2026-06-09

Security+ SY0-701 Path

v2.5 adds a complete CompTIA Security+ SY0-701 curriculum path to BREACH.MMO. Fifteen missions mapped to official exam objectives, three new challenge types built around the domains where players most often fail the exam, and a dashboard panel that shows your coverage across all five SY0-701 domains. Complete every domain, earn the Security+ Ready badge.

The new challenge types each target a different gap in Security+ preparation. SecurityPolicyChallenge puts you in front of a compliance audit — select the controls that satisfy the standard and justify why the others fall short. ThreatClassificationChallenge gives you raw threat intel: classify the actor, map the tactic to ATT&CK, attribute with evidence. Wrong classifications teach you the difference between the real exam options. IncidentResponseChallenge runs you through a PICERL-sequenced incident — pick the containment action, then eradication, then recovery, in the right order. The write-ups cite the SY0-701 objective number so you can cross-reference with your study materials.

The Security+ path is gated behind the Analyst Pass. Free-tier players can see the landing page at /breach/security-plus and the domain tracker — the upsell is visible but not intrusive. The path is designed to complement a study schedule, not replace it. BREACH gives you the pattern recognition; the exam tests the vocabulary.
